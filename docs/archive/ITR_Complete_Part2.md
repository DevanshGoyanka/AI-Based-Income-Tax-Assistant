## SECTION 6 — ITR-3 — COMPLETE FIELD GUIDE

### 6.1 Who Files ITR-3

- Individuals/HUFs with income from business or profession (NOT covered under 44AD/44ADA/44AE)
- Partners in a partnership firm
- Those opting OUT of presumptive scheme
- F&O (Futures and Options) traders — treated as non-speculative business
- Anyone with business income + any other head of income
- If turnover exceeds 44AD/44ADA limits but has business income

### 6.2 Schedule BP — Business or Professional Income

**Computation Starting from P&L Account:**

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Net Profit/(Loss) as per P&L Account | From books of accounts (audited or unaudited) |
| Add: Personal / inadmissible expenses debited to P&L | Personal withdrawals, club expenses for personal use, etc. |
| Add: Capital expenditure debited to P&L | CAPEX wrongly charged to revenue |
| Add: Excess depreciation over books | If books claim more depreciation than IT Act allows |
| Add: Disallowed payments u/s 40A(3) | Cash payments > ₹10,000 per day per person |
| Add: Disallowed employee PF/ESI contributions | If deposited after due date u/s 36(1)(va) |
| Add: Disallowed expenditure u/s 14A | Expenses attributable to exempt income (Rule 8D) |
| Add: Disallowance u/s 43B | Payments not made before filing date |
| Less: Income taxable under other heads | Dividend, rental income, capital gains — remove to avoid double counting |
| Less: IT Act depreciation (Schedule DPM) | Replace book depreciation with IT Act depreciation |
| Less: Additional depreciation u/s 32(1)(iia) | 20% in year of installation for eligible assets |
| Less: Any other permissible deduction | Investment allowance, donations to approved bodies, etc. |
| **Profit and Gains from Business/Profession** | CALCULATED — Final figure |

| Interest on loan from public financial institutions | 43B(d) | Must be paid before filing date |

### 6.5 Section 14A — Disallowance for Exempt Income

```
If an assessee earns exempt income (dividends before AY 2021-22, PPF interest, etc.),
expenses incurred TO EARN that exempt income are DISALLOWED.

Rule 8D Formula (if A.O. is not satisfied with assessee's own calculation):
  Disallowance = A + B

Where:
  A = Interest expenditure attributable to earning exempt income
    = (Average value of investments earning exempt income / Average total assets) × Total interest expenditure

  B = 1% of average value of investments earning exempt income
  
  Total 14A disallowance cannot exceed total expenditure claimed
  
NOTE: If there is NO exempt income during the year, 14A disallowance = NIL
NOTE: From AY 2022-23 (Budget 2022): Dividend is now fully taxable, so 14A rarely applies for listed shares
Still applies for: PPF interest, LIC maturity proceeds, tax-free bonds, share in firm's profit (10(2A))
```

### 6.6 Depreciation — Complete Reference (Schedule DPM)

**Block of Assets Method:**

```
Block Opening WDV
  Add: Cost of new assets acquired during the year
  Less: Sale proceeds of assets sold during the year (max = opening WDV + additions)

If (Sale proceeds) > (Opening WDV + Additions):
  → Entire block becomes zero
  → Excess of sale proceeds = SHORT-TERM CAPITAL GAIN (no depreciation on this block)

Depreciation = Closing WDV × Rate

If asset acquired in second half of year (after October 1):
  Depreciation = 50% of normal rate
```

**IT Act Depreciation Rates:**

| Asset Category | Rate |
|---|---|
| Buildings (RCC — Residential) | 5% |
| Buildings (RCC — Non-residential) | 10% |
| Temporary structures | 40% |
| Furniture and fittings | 10% |
| Plant and machinery (general) | 15% |
| Computers, peripherals, software, printers | 40% |
| Motor cars (not plying for hire) | 15% |
| Motor buses/lorries for hire | 30% |
| Aircraft, ships | 20% |
| Books (own by assessee for business) | 100% |
| Patents, copyrights, trademarks, know-how, licenses | 25% |
| Goodwill | **0% (Budget 2021: goodwill NOT depreciable)** |
| Windmills and specific energy assets | 40% |
| Energy-saving devices | 40% |
| New P&M (additional depreciation) | 20% in year 1 (for eligible assets) |

**Goodwill (From AY 2021-22):** Budget 2021 amended Section 32 to exclude goodwill from the block of assets. No depreciation on goodwill acquired on or after April 1, 2021. Goodwill on business combination post April 1, 2021 = not eligible for depreciation.

### 6.8 F&O (Futures and Options) — Complete Treatment

| Aspect | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Classification | Non-Speculative Business Income u/s 43(5) proviso — always |
| ITR Form Required | ITR-3 (even if salaried and F&O is secondary activity) |
| Turnover Definition (Futures) | Absolute profit + Absolute loss on all settled contracts |
| Turnover Definition (Options — Buy/Sell) | Premium received on sale of options + Absolute profit/loss on exercised/expired options |
| Turnover (for Audit threshold) | As above |
| Audit u/s 44AB | If F&O turnover > ₹10 crore (digital transactions); or > ₹1 crore (if cash > 5%); OR if profit < 6% of turnover and income > basic exemption |
| Loss Set-Off | Against any income EXCEPT salary (current year) |
| Loss Carry Forward | 8 years — only against business income |
| STT Deduction | STT paid on F&O transactions is a deductible business expense |
| Expenses Deductible | Brokerage, platform charges, SEBI fees, internet, office, depreciation on trading computer |
| TDS | No TDS on F&O settlement — only on contract notes |

**F&O Turnover Computation Example:**
```
Trade 1: Nifty Futures — Buy ₹15,00,000, Sell ₹15,50,000 → Profit ₹50,000 → Absolute ₹50,000
Trade 2: Bank Nifty — Buy ₹10,00,000, Sell ₹9,50,000 → Loss ₹50,000 → Absolute ₹50,000
Trade 3: Nifty Option Sold at ₹15,000 premium → absolute ₹15,000

Turnover = ₹50,000 + ₹50,000 + ₹15,000 = ₹1,15,000

NOTE: Turnover for audit purposes ≠ total contracts value
```

### 6.9 Partner's Income from Firm

| Income Component | Tax Treatment in Partner's Hands |
|---|---|
| Share of profit from firm | EXEMPT u/s 10(2A) — not taxable |
| Interest on capital from firm | Taxable as business income (max 12% p.a. per firm deed) |
| Salary/remuneration from firm | Taxable as business income (limits: see below) |
| Loan repayment from firm | Not taxable (return of capital) |

**Firm Deduction Limits for Remuneration u/s 40(b):**
```
Maximum deductible remuneration to working partners:
  On first ₹3,00,000 of book profit (or where book profit is a loss): 90% of book profit or ₹1,50,000 (whichever higher)
  On balance of book profit: 60%

This is the FIRM's limit. The partner must declare the received amount in ITR-3.
If firm pays more than limit → excess disallowed in firm's computation; but still taxable in partner's hands.
```

### 6.10 Schedule 80JJAA — New Employee Deduction

Available to employers for eligible new employees hired:

```
Deduction = 30% of additional employee cost for 3 consecutive AYs

Conditions:
  1. Business must be subject to tax audit (turnover > ₹1 crore / ₹50L for professionals)
  2. New employee = hired during the year; not previously employed by the assessee
  3. Employee's monthly emoluments ≤ ₹25,000
  4. Employee must work for at least 240 days in the year (150 days for apparel/footwear/leather)
  5. Emoluments paid via banking channel (not cash)
  6. Employee must be registered with EPFO

NOT available for:
  - Government employees transferred
  - Employees whose emoluments not included in 44AB audit
  
Available in NEW REGIME: YES (one of the few deductions available in new regime)
```

### 7.2 Section 44AD — Presumptive Business Income

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Applicable to | Any business EXCEPT transport (44AE), commission agent, brokerage |
| Turnover limit | ≤ ₹2 crore (or ≤ ₹3 crore if BOTH receipts AND payments in cash ≤ 5% each) |
| Presumptive income (digital/non-cash) | **6%** of gross turnover |
| Presumptive income (cash transactions) | **8%** of gross turnover |
| Split calculation | 6% on digital receipts + 8% on cash receipts = aggregate income |
| Can declare higher? | YES — any amount ≥ presumptive rate |
| Can declare lower? | ONLY with books and audit u/s 44AB |
| Advance tax | 100% by March 15 (single installment) |
| Books required? | NOT required if opting |
| Further deductions (30-38) | NOT available — all deemed included |

**44AD Turnover Definition:**
```
Turnover = All gross sales / receipts from the business

Digital = NEFT, RTGS, UPI, cheque, DD, IMPS, card
Cash = physical cash received/paid

5% test (independent for BOTH):
  Cash receipts / Total receipts ≤ 5%  AND
  Cash payments / Total payments ≤ 5%
  If BOTH ≤ 5% → limit is ₹3 crore
  If EITHER > 5% → limit is ₹2 crore
```

**44AD Opt-Out Lock-In:**
```
If 44AD claimed in any year, then for next 5 consecutive years:
  → Must continue claiming 44AD, OR
  → If turnover exceeds limit / opts out voluntarily:
     Must maintain books u/s 44AA
     Must get audit u/s 44AB for those 5 years
     
This is a SIGNIFICANT lock-in. ERP MUST flag this to clients.
```

### 7.3 Section 44ADA — Presumptive Professional Income

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Applicable professions | Legal, Medical, Engineering, Architecture, Accounting, Technical consulting, Interior decoration, Film artist, and notified professions |
| Gross receipts limit | ≤ ₹50 lakh (or ≤ ₹75 lakh if BOTH cash receipts AND payments ≤ 5%) |
| Presumptive income rate | **50%** of gross receipts |
| Can declare higher? | YES |
| Can declare lower? | Only with books and audit |
| Advance tax | 100% by March 15 |
| Books | NOT required |
| Further deductions | NOT available |
| Partnership firm of professionals | NOT eligible — only individuals |
| LLP professionals | NOT eligible |

**44ADA Eligible / Not Eligible Computation:**
```
If gross receipts = ₹70,00,000 with 3% cash:
  Cash ≤ 5% → eligible up to ₹75 lakh limit → 44ADA applicable
  Presumptive income = 50% × ₹70,00,000 = ₹35,00,000

If gross receipts = ₹60,00,000 with 8% cash:
  Cash > 5% → limit = ₹50 lakh
  ₹60L > ₹50L → NOT eligible for 44ADA → must maintain books → file ITR-3
```

### 7.4 Section 44AE — Goods Vehicle Operators

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Applicable to | Plying, hiring, leasing goods vehicles (NOT passenger vehicles) |
| Vehicle limit | ≤ 10 goods vehicles at any time during the year |
| Heavy goods vehicle (>12T GVW) | ₹1,000 per ton of gross vehicle weight per month |
| Non-heavy goods vehicle (≤12T GVW) | ₹7,500 per vehicle per month |
| Part month = full month | Any part of a month counted as full |
| Can declare higher? | YES |
| Advance tax | 100% by March 15 |

**44AE Computation Example:**
```
3 heavy goods vehicles: 18 tonnes each, owned for 12 months
Presumptive income = 3 × 12 × 18 × ₹1,000 = ₹6,48,000

If any vehicle sold/bought mid-year: count months of ownership only
If vehicle owned 7 months: 7 months × rate
```

### 8.4 TDS Computation u/s 192 — Employer's Obligation

```
Employer must compute estimated annual tax liability of each employee:

Step 1: Estimate gross salary for the year
Step 2: Less: exempt allowances (HRA, LTA, etc.)
Step 3: Less: Standard deduction (₹50,000 or ₹75,000 per regime)
Step 4: Less: Professional tax, entertainment allowance
Step 5: Add: Other income disclosed by employee (interest, HP income, etc.)
Step 6: Less: Chapter VI-A deductions declared by employee (80C, 80D, etc.) — OLD REGIME ONLY
Step 7: Compute total income
Step 8: Compute income tax + surcharge + cess on total income
Step 9: Less: TDS already deducted so far in the year
```

### 10.3 Section 50C — Stamp Duty Value (Detailed)

```
For immovable property sale:
  If Actual Sale Price < Stamp Duty Value (SDV/Circle Rate):
    Deemed Sale Consideration = SDV
    
  Exception: If SDV ≤ 110% of actual sale price → actual price accepted
  
  If dispute regarding SDV: Assessee may request A.O. for FMV determination by a Valuation Officer
  
  For BUYER (Section 56(2)(x)):
    If SDV > 110% of purchase price → excess is taxable as Other Sources in buyer's hands
    Buyer must report this in Schedule OS
    
  Property not registered during FY:
    If possession given under 53A agreement: Transfer deemed to have occurred
    SDV at time of agreement applies (not at time of actual registration)
    
  Section 50CA (Unlisted shares):
    If sale consideration < FMV of unlisted shares → FMV is deemed consideration
    FMV computed as per Rule 11UA (NAV method for unlisted shares)
```

### 10.4 Intraday Trading — Speculative Business

```
Intraday equity trading = SPECULATIVE BUSINESS income (u/s 43(5))

Turnover for intraday = Absolute profit + Absolute loss (settlement value differences)
(Only the settlement/squared-off difference — NOT gross sale value)

Tax rate: Slab rate (as business income)
Loss: Can set off only against speculative business income in current year
Carry forward: 4 years, only against speculative income

ITR required: ITR-3 (even if also salaried)
Audit required: If turnover > ₹1 crore (same rules as F&O)

Difference from F&O:
  F&O = NON-SPECULATIVE (u/s 43(5) proviso — exceptions)
  Intraday = SPECULATIVE
```

### 10.5 Capital Gains on Property — Complete Computation (AY 2025-26)

**Property acquired BEFORE July 23, 2024 (choice available):**
```
Option A: 20% tax WITH indexation
  Indexed Cost = Purchase Price × (363 / CII of purchase year)
  Taxable LTCG = Sale Price − Transfer expenses − Indexed Cost
  Tax = 20% × Taxable LTCG

Option B: 12.5% tax WITHOUT indexation
  Taxable LTCG = Sale Price − Transfer expenses − Actual Cost
  Tax = 12.5% × Taxable LTCG

ERP must compute BOTH and offer the option that results in LOWER tax
Taxpayer declares their choice in ITR

NOTE: If property transferred for deferred consideration (installments):
  Full consideration = All installments including future ones
  Tax payable upfront on full amount
```

**Property acquired AFTER July 23, 2024:**
```
Only ONE option: 12.5% WITHOUT indexation
Taxable LTCG = Sale Price − Transfer expenses − Actual Cost (no indexation)
Tax = 12.5%
```

**Transfer Expenses Eligible for Deduction:**
- Brokerage/commission paid to broker for selling property
- Legal fees, stamp duty on transfer to buyer
- Advertisement expenses for sale
- Society transfer charges
- TDS u/s 194IA (1%) — this TDS is an advance tax, NOT a transfer expense

**AY 2026-27 Marginal Relief Computation for ₹12L Cliff:**
```
For income between ₹12,00,001 and approximately ₹12,75,000 (new regime):
  Tax without rebate = Full slab tax on actual income
  Income over ₹12L = Actual Income − ₹12,00,000
  
  If Tax without rebate > Income over ₹12L:
    Effective tax payable = Income − ₹12,00,000 (capped)
  
Example: Income = ₹12,50,000
  Tax without rebate = (₹4L × 0%) + (₹4L × 5%) + (₹4L × 10%) + (₹50K × 15%)
                     = 0 + 20,000 + 40,000 + 7,500 = ₹67,500
  Income over ₹12L = ₹50,000
  Since ₹67,500 > ₹50,000 → Tax capped at ₹50,000 (marginal relief applies)
  
  After marginal relief: Tax = ₹50,000
  Cess: 4% of ₹50,000 = ₹2,000
  Total Tax: ₹52,000
```

### 12.4 Critical Edge Cases — Comprehensive Reference

| Edge Case | Correct Treatment |
|---|---|
| Income at exactly ₹7,00,000 (new regime AY 25-26) | 87A rebate applies — tax = NIL. ₹7,00,001 → NO rebate → full tax ~₹25,010. ERP MUST WARN. |
| Income at exactly ₹12,00,000 (new regime AY 26-27) | 87A rebate applies — tax = NIL. ₹12,00,001 → tax ~₹61,500 but marginal relief kicks in (tax capped at ₹1). |
| ₹5L rebate cliff (old regime) | ₹5,00,001 → full slab tax with no rebate. ERP MUST WARN. |
| Surcharge threshold cliff | Marginal relief at ₹50L, ₹1Cr, ₹2Cr, ₹5Cr — mandatory computation |
| Negative GTI | GTI = 0; Tax = 0; carry forward losses per rules |
| TDS > tax liability | Refund; no 234A/B/C; add bank account for refund |
| Two employers — double standard deduction | Only ONE standard deduction across all employers. Correct in ITR. |
| Property sale and 54/54EC investment straddles FYs | Deposit in CGAS before ITR due date to preserve exemption |
| HP loss > ₹2L with only salary — ITR-1 | File ITR-2 to carry forward excess HP loss. ITR-1 CANNOT carry forward. |
| NPS withdrawal on retirement (60%) | Exempt u/s 10(12A). Balance 40% → must purchase annuity → annuity taxable later. |
| EPF withdrawal after 5 years of service | EXEMPT u/s 10(12). Before 5 years → taxable + TDS u/s 192A at 10%. |
| ESOP exercise + same year sale | Perquisite taxable as salary at exercise. Capital gain on subsequent sale from exercise price (cost = FMV on exercise). |
| Salary received from two countries | Declare both; apply DTAA for double tax relief |
| Gift of property to spouse | CG exempt u/s 47(iii). But income from that property clubbed in transferor's hands u/s 64(1)(iv). |
| Minor's FD interest | Taxable in higher-earning parent's hands; ₹1,500 annual exemption per minor. |
| Death of taxpayer mid-year | Legal heirs file ITR for deceased. Income from April 1 to date of death = deceased's income. Income after death = estate/heir's income. |
| Advance tax in wrong AY | Correct via NSDL within 3 months or 6 months (depending on bank). If not corrected: 234B risk; application for rectification u/s 154. |
| Intraday loss + F&O profit | Intraday = speculative loss (set off against speculative income only). F&O = non-speculative (set off against any income except salary). They CANNOT offset each other if F&O is profit and intraday is loss. |
| Agricultural income + non-agricultural income | Partial integration: compute tax on (normal + agri income), then deduct tax on (agri income + basic exemption). File ITR-2 if agri > ₹5,000. |
| 80C investment but belated return | YES — 80C deduction available on belated return. Only loss carry forward denied. |
| NSC interest — 80C and income | NSC accrued interest = Other Sources income. The same amount also qualifies for 80C reinvestment in old regime. Net impact can be zero if 80C limit not exhausted. |

| VDA received as gift | If from non-relative and FMV > ₹50,000 → taxable u/s 56(2)(x) at FMV. Then cost for future sale = FMV at time of gift. |
| Indexation for property bought from parent | Use PARENT's original cost and PARENT's acquisition year for indexation (stepped-up from when parent acquired). |
| Section 54F — multiple reinvestments | 54F requires reinvestment in ONE residential property. Multiple partial investments in different properties → proportionate relief only for the one qualifying house. |
